# Bible Read

A reading-habit app: people read scripture daily, on their own or alongside others,
and the app records that they showed up.

## Language

**Plan**:
A schedule of daily readings a person follows. Either solo or shared; the only
difference is who else follows the same schedule.
_Avoid_: Reading plan, personal plan, challenge

**Shared plan**:
A Plan whose schedule is followed by a Group. Not a different kind of Plan —
only its audience differs.
_Avoid_: Group plan, group schedule

**Group**:
The set of people following a Shared plan together.
_Avoid_: Community, team

**Circle**:
Everyone you share at least one Group with. Derived from Group membership,
never established directly — there is no separate mutual-connection graph.
_Avoid_: Friend, contact, follower

**Nudge**:
A gentle prompt one person sends another in their Circle who has not shown up
today. It invites them to read; it never refers to a schedule they are behind
on, and it is not tied to any one Group.
_Avoid_: Poke, reminder, ping

**Progress**:
How far one person has got through a Plan. Always per-person, never pooled,
even on a Shared plan — but every member of a Group can see every other
member's Progress.
_Avoid_: Completion, group progress

**Showing up**:
Marking that you spent time in the Word today. Independent of any Plan —
it counts whether or not you followed your schedule.
_Avoid_: Check-in, streak entry, marking as read
