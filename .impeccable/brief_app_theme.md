# Surface Brief: Bible Read Global Visual System

- **Scope**: Entire App Visual System & Theme
- **Mode**: operate
- **Audience & Job**: Individuals and community groups building daily Bible reading habits without guilt or pressure.
- **Task**: Daily reading check-in, reviewing group activity (Circle), and managing reading plans and coverage (Path).
- **Constraints**: Flutter Material 3 adaptive architecture for iOS and Android; WCAG 2.2 touch targets (48x48 dp / 44x44 pt); 1000ms minimum skeleton loaders with persistent headers; optimistic UI updates.

## Direction contract

**THESIS**: Daily Bible habit tracking mapped to the gentle rhythm of canonical light, refusing gamified streak alarms, punitive red counters, and loud commercial UI clutter.

**OWN-WORLD**: Palette of warm limestone (#F4F0E8 light ground), deep aubergine stone (#1E1A24 dark ground), vesper lavender (#6A53AD primary), and beeswax gold (#D4A359 accent). A tactile gold check-in button provides immediate presence marking, flanked by a quiet consistency glimpse and quick catch-up access.
