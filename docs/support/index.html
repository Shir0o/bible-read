Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do daily check-ins and reading streaks work?

Check in daily by marking your readings as completed. Your streak tracks consecutive days you show up to read. If you are enrolled in a reading plan with "Reading counts as showing up" enabled in Settings, completing a plan reading will also maintain your daily streak.

Will my reading progress sync across multiple devices?

Yes. When signed in via Apple, Google, or Email/Password, all your reading plans, book progress, streaks, and reflections sync automatically in real time through our secure cloud backend.

How do group reading challenges work?

You can create or join reading groups from the Groups tab. Members can share reflections, encourage one another, view progress feeds, and stay accountable together.

How do I manage push notifications and daily reminders?

Open the app, navigate to Settings > Notification settings, where you can toggle daily reminder alerts, group activity notifications, and set your preferred reminder time.

How can I delete my account and data?

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly in the app under Settings > Account > Delete Account, or via our web Account Deletion Request page.
