Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Bible Reading Challenge  ·  Last updated: September 10, 2026

At Bible Reading Challenge, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion using either of the methods below:

Option 1: Web / Email Request (Without Installing or Logging into the App)

If you uninstalled the app or no longer have access to it:

  1. Send an email to twangdeveloper@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request - Bible Reading Challenge.
  2. Send the email from the email address registered with your account, or specify your registered email address and display name.
  3. Your request will be processed and all personal data deleted within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once completed.

Option 2: In-App Request

If you have the app installed on your device:

  1. Open Bible Reading Challenge.
  2. Go to your Profile or Settings screen.
  3. Select Delete Account (or submit an account deletion request via Settings / Feedback).
  4. Confirm your deletion request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following information is permanently removed from our Firebase systems:

  • Account & Authentication: Your Firebase Authentication user account, user ID, email address, display name, and profile picture.
  • Reading History & Streaks: All daily reading logs, streak counts, read-throughs, Bible book check-offs, and custom reading plans.
  • Social & Community Data: Comments, likes/reactions, friend associations, and group memberships created by your account.
  • Notification Tokens: Device push notification tokens stored in Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

Data Retention & Exceptions

We do not retain personal identifying information once deletion is completed. The only exceptions are:

  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Anonymized technical logs collected via Firebase Crashlytics are retained for up to 90 days before automatic expiration, used solely for app stability.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Minimal audit records may be retained only if strictly necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations or fraud-prevention requirements.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this deletion process or your data rights, please contact us at: twangdeveloper@gmail.com.
