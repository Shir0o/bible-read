Privacy Policy

Bible Reading Challenge  ·  Last updated: September 10, 2026

Bible Reading Challenge ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated by your use of the app:

  • Account information – name, email address, and profile photo when you sign in with Google or create an email/password account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Reading activity – daily reading check-ins, streak counts, Bible book progress, and challenge participation stored in Firestore.
  • Social interactions – likes, comments, and friend connections you make within the app.
  • Device & usage data – crash reports and error logs collected automatically via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs.
  • Push notification tokens – device tokens used to deliver notifications you've opted into via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize the app experience (reading feeds, streaks, challenges).
  • Send push notifications you've enabled (e.g., reading reminders, likes, comments, friend requests).
  • Diagnose crashes and improve app stability using Crashlytics logs.
  • Display your reading activity to friends or group members you're connected with.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these limited cases:

  • Firebase / Google – our backend infrastructure. Data is processed under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • Other users – your display name, profile photo, and reading activity are visible to friends and group members as part of the community features.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below. Crash logs are retained for up to 90 days.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Choices

  • Notifications – you can manage notification preferences in the app's Settings screen.
  • Account deletion – contact us to request full deletion of your account and data.
  • Data access – contact us to request a copy of your personal data.

7. Security

We use Firebase App Check and industry-standard security practices to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to safeguard your information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via the app or by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at:
